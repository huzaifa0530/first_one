About PSN

The Pakistan Society of Nephrology

Founded in 1995, the Pakistan Society of Nephrology is a professional organization committed to advancing the care of patients with kidney diseases through education, research, and advocacy. PSN has worked tirelessly to improve nephrology care across Pakistan, fostering collaboration among healthcare professionals and supporting the latest advancements in kidney health.
